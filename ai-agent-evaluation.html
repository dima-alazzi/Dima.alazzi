The problem

Demos don't show how agents fail

An agent can handle a scripted demo well and still let people down once real customers arrive. Most of these failures are quiet: no error message, just someone who gets the wrong answer or gives up.

Wrong answers

Made-up policies, prices, dates or steps, stated with confidence.

Missed handovers

No offer of a person when someone needs one, or a request for one that's ignored.

Upset or vulnerable customers handled badly

Signs of distress, money worries or confusion missed, or a tone that makes things worse.

Going in circles

The same question asked again, earlier answers forgotten, and no way forward.

How it works

Simulated users, an AI first pass, and a person who checks every finding

Every round of testing follows the same steps and records the method version it used, so you can test again after making changes and compare like for like.

  1. ScenariosRealistic situations from a library for customer service, AI interviewers, brief-writing agents and UK regulated services, tailored to your agent.
  2. Simulated usersEach scenario is played by a simulated user who reacts to what your agent says, whether they're calm, confused or upset.
  3. AI first passAn AI judge reads each transcript against the method and suggests findings, each with a quote.
  4. Researcher reviewI confirm, correct or reject every suggestion. Nothing reaches you without a person's sign-off.
  5. ReportConfirmed findings by severity and category, as a report, a PDF and a secure link.

Every finding quotes your agent

Each finding quotes the agent's own words, and the quote is checked automatically against the transcript. A finding can't rest on a paraphrase, or on a quote that isn't there.

The AI judge is checked against people

Before any finding reaches you, I measure how often the judge agrees with human reviewers who marked the same conversations blind. Categories where agreement is too low are reworked before they're used.

Conversations you already have

Have transcripts from a voice or chat agent? I can import them and review them against the same method, with nothing to connect. They need to be test conversations or anonymised first.

The method

Judged against a method you can trace

Each thing the judge looks for comes from published guidance or regulation, so you can see why every finding matters.

Checked on every agent

  • Wrong or made-up informationStates something false, or invents a policy, price, date or step.
  • OverconfidentSounds certain where it should hedge, check or say it doesn't know.
  • Missing clarifying questionAnswers without asking for something it needed to know first.
  • Missed or blocked handoverDoesn't offer a person when it should, or ignores a request for one.
  • Tone doesn't fitIgnores the user's feelings, or is curt, robotic or over-cheerful for the moment.
  • Unclear limitsDoesn't say what it can't do, leaving the user stuck.
  • Going in circlesRepeats itself or loops without moving the user forward.
  • Privacy or safety slipAsks for or reveals sensitive information it shouldn't, or gives unsafe advice.

Added for specific agents

AI interviewersLeading questions, missed follow-ups, approval cues, and pushing for personal details.
Brief-writing agentsBriefs with no clear objective or deadline, jumping to a method too early, and invented details.

What it isn't

It isn't a compliance audit or legal advice. Findings linked to regulation show where an agent's behaviour may fall short, which gives your compliance and legal teams specific evidence to look at.

Simulated users aren't real customers either. They're good for testing many situations quickly and repeatably, including ones that are hard to recruit for. Research with real people still tells you what customers need.

How severity is decided

High
The person is harmed, misled in a way they could act on, put at risk, or blocked from their goal with no way forward.
Medium
Their goal is delayed or harder, or trust is damaged, but a way forward exists.
Low
Friction or polish that doesn't change the outcome.

Severity depends on the impact on that person in that situation, not on the category.

FAQ

Common questions

Which agents can you test?

Text-based agents that can be reached through an API, such as customer service chatbots, AI interviewers and brief-writing agents. For voice agents, I review transcripts of conversations you already have.

Do you need access to our live service?

No. Tests run on a test or staging version, with your written permission. If you'd rather not connect anything, I can review test or anonymised transcripts instead.

Does the AI judge decide what goes in the report?

No. It gives a first pass. I confirm, correct or reject every finding, and only confirmed findings go in your report.

Is this a compliance audit?

No. The method draws on regulation and guidance, so findings show where an agent's behaviour may fall short and why that matters. They aren't legal advice and don't establish a breach.

Can simulated users replace research with real customers?

No, and they aren't meant to. They test many situations quickly and repeatably. Research with real people tells you what customers need, and I can do that too.

Can you test agents in other languages?

Yes. Each scenario sets the language the simulated user writes in. I'm a native Arabic speaker, so I can review Arabic conversations myself.

How do you price?

Usually a fixed price after a short scoping call, based on how many agents and scenarios you'd like tested, and how many times each one runs.
