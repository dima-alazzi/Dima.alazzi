AI enablement

Faster research, still done properly

Most teams have tried an AI summary of a transcript. Far fewer have rebuilt their research workflow around AI in a way they can trust. That second part is where I come in.

  • I've led AI adoption inside a regulated bank. As my team's first Copilot Champion at Barclays, I piloted AI-assisted research workflows and automation, and trained colleagues to build their own agents.
  • I build as well as advise. Applied AI and machine learning training, including an NLP model built with spaCy and Prodigy, plus Power Automate and Make workflows and working prototypes like SigQ, an agent that turns vague requests into structured briefs.
  • Responsible by default. I contributed to Sky's internal guidance on using generative AI responsibly in research. In everything I build, people keep the judgement and every insight traces back to its evidence.

Selected work

Research that shaped products, services and strategy

A sample of recent and earlier work. Client work is anonymised where confidentiality requires it.

Wealth managementDiscovery & evaluationBehavioural insight

Financial advice & decision confidence

People managing their money want control, but also reassurance at key moments. I led end-to-end discovery and evaluative research across digital advice and investment journeys, looking at how advice cues, language and prompts affect confidence, trust and perceived risk.

Outcome: shaped experience strategy and priorities, adding guidance where it builds confidence and stepping back where people prefer autonomy.
Wealth managementMental modelsJourney research

Digital investing journeys

Investing tools often assume rational behaviour, yet emotional confidence decides how people act. I researched mental models of autonomy, guidance and reassurance across entry points, risk levels and journey stages.

Outcome: made clear when people want self-directed control and when they want guided support, informing different design and content strategies for each.
Regulated enterpriseDiscoveryGovernance

Discovery for a compliance dashboard

Internal platforms in regulated firms must support oversight and audit readiness, not just efficiency. I led discovery into accountability, exception handling and status visibility, framing insights through a governance and risk lens.

Outcome: steered early product direction towards transparency, oversight and regulatory resilience.
ResearchOpsStrategyExecutive dashboards

Turning research into decision-ready intelligence

Senior leaders couldn't see how research lined up with strategy, timing and investment. I designed an enablement framework: a strategic research roadmap, capacity forecasting across research streams and a leadership dashboard that turns research activity into executive signals.

Outcome: clearer prioritisation and resourcing, less reactive ad-hoc research, and research brought earlier into strategic decisions.
DemoAI agentsConversational UXAutomation

SigQ: an AI agent for sharper briefs

Vague requests create friction before work even starts. I designed and prototyped conversational AI agents that ask targeted clarifying questions and turn a rough request into a structured brief, automatically saved to Airtable via Make.

Outcome: showed how AI can raise the quality of requests upstream while supporting, not replacing, researcher judgement.

About

Hi, I'm Dima.

I study how people and technology evolve together, and help teams make better decisions because of it.

I'm a research lead with more than ten years' experience shaping evidence-led decisions in wealth and financial services, connected entertainment, media, charities and international development. Most recently, at Barclays, I led the research roadmap for Wealth Planning & Advice and Digital Investing, bringing client and adviser evidence into senior decisions on retirement, pensions, SIPP and ISA propositions.

Before that I was Senior UX Researcher at Sky, leading research for connected-entertainment products in the UK, US, Italy and Germany. Earlier, as Senior Research Manager at the BBC, I led research across Ethiopia, Sierra Leone and Bangladesh and conceived a multilingual conversational AI chatbot backed by a central insight repository.

I've also done research where it's hardest to do: with displaced communities for the UN's IOM during the Syrian conflict, at Mencap, where I built and led the research and evaluation team, and with farmers in rural Kenya as a startup co-founder. That work taught me to build empathy quickly, adapt methods to the people involved and turn messy reality into clear decisions.

I founded my consultancy to bring that experience, together with hands-on AI and automation skills, to teams that want to do better research and get more value from it.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you work outside IR35?

Yes. I contract through my own limited company and I'm happy to agree terms, a statement of work and working practices that support an outside-IR35 determination.

Have you worked in regulated industries?

Yes. I've led research in wealth management at Barclays, working closely with proposition, product, design, compliance and delivery teams, and I hold a Risk & Control Accreditation. I'm used to the ethics, data protection and approval processes that high-stakes research needs.

Can you work with our existing research team or agency?

Yes. I often embed alongside in-house researchers, or act as the client-side lead who briefs, manages and quality-assures agency partners.

Which markets and languages can you cover?

I've run research in the UK, Italy, Germany, the US, Kenya, Ethiopia, Sierra Leone, Bangladesh and Syria. I'm a native Arabic speaker and can lead Arabic-language and Middle East research directly.

Is AI-assisted research safe for our participants' data?

Data protection is built into everything I set up. I'll recommend tools and setups that fit your data policies, keep personal data out of public models and document how AI is used, so stakeholders can trust the output.

How do you price?

Projects are usually fixed-price after a short scoping call. Contracts are charged by the day, and advisory work is a monthly retainer. Get in touch for a tailored quote.
