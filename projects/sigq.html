SigQ

AI agent concept that transforms vague stakeholder requests into structured product briefs.

Concept

SigQ is a concept AI agent designed to help teams translate early ideas into clear and structured product briefs. Instead of submitting incomplete requests, stakeholders interact with the agent through a short conversation that clarifies objectives, metrics, and context.

How it works

  • User submits a high-level request
  • The agent asks targeted clarification questions
  • The brief becomes structured and actionable
  • The final output is automatically stored in Airtable via Make automation
