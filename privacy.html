Privacy notice

Last updated: November 2026

Who I am

This website is run by Dima Alazzi, an independent research and AI consultant working through a UK limited company. For anything privacy-related, email dimaalazzi@icloud.com.

What I collect

If you send an enquiry through the contact form or by email, I receive your name, email address, company (if you give it) and whatever you write in your message.

To see how the site is used, I count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't identify or follow individual visitors. It records things like the page you viewed, the site that sent you here, your browser and device type, and your approximate country. The site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How I use it

Only to reply to your enquiry and, if we decide to work together, to agree and deliver that work. The lawful basis is my legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries, or taking steps you've asked for before entering into a contract. I never sell your data or add you to a mailing list.

Who else handles it

  • Web3Forms delivers contact-form messages to my inbox.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log visitors' IP addresses for security.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits to this site, without cookies.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.
  • YouTube hosts the SigQ demo video, embedded in privacy-enhanced mode. YouTube may store data on your device if you play it.
  • My email provider stores the messages I receive.

Some of these providers process data outside the UK, including in the US.

How long I keep it

Enquiries that don't lead to work are deleted within 12 months. If we work together, I keep business records for as long as the law requires, usually six years after the end of the financial year they relate to.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data I hold about you, object to or restrict how I use it, or ask for a copy to take elsewhere. Email me and I'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
